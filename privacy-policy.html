Privacy Policy for Lokd In

Last Updated: November 9, 2025

TL;DR: Lokd In is a privacy-first focus app. All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or share any personal information on external servers. Your camera is only used locally for focus tracking - no images are ever saved or transmitted.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lokd In ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Don't Collect

Lokd In does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Images or videos from your camera
  • Contacts or other device data

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

3.1 Session History

We store records of your focus sessions, including:

  • Session duration and target time
  • Focus quality scores
  • Warning and focus lost counts
  • Session completion dates and times
  • Sensitivity settings used

Storage Method: AsyncStorage (local device storage only)
Purpose: To display your session history and track your progress
Your Control: You can delete all session history at any time through the Settings screen

3.2 App Settings

We store your preferences locally, including:

  • Default timer duration
  • Focus sensitivity level (low/medium/high)
  • Notification, sound, and haptic preferences
  • Face and eye tracking toggles
  • Focus Friend contact information (optional)

4. Permissions We Request

4.1 Camera Access

Purpose: To enable optional face and eye tracking features that detect when you look away from the screen or close your eyes.

How It Works:

  • Camera data is processed in real-time on your device only
  • We analyze head angle and eye state using device-based face detection
  • No images or videos are ever saved, stored, or transmitted
  • All processing happens locally and immediately discards camera data

Your Control: Camera access is optional. You can enable/disable face and eye tracking in Settings at any time.

4.2 Notifications

Purpose: To send you local reminders about your focus sessions.

How It Works:

  • All notifications are generated locally on your device
  • No push notification service or external server is used
  • Notifications are sent only when you complete a session (if enabled)

Your Control: You can disable notifications in Settings or through your device's system settings.

4.3 SMS Access (Optional)

Purpose: To enable the "Focus Friend" feature, which allows you to share session results.

How It Works:

  • The App opens your device's default SMS app with a pre-filled message
  • You must manually review and send the message
  • The App does not automatically send SMS messages
  • We do not store or access your text message history

Your Control: This feature is entirely optional. You choose if and when to use it.

5. Third-Party Services

Lokd In does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App operates completely offline after installation.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to external servers:

  • Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features
  • We recommend using device encryption and password/biometric locks
  • If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is deleted

7. Children's Privacy

Lokd In does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The App is designed for general audiences and does not require any personal information to use.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date and may provide additional notice within the App.

9. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Access: View your session history anytime in the App
  • Delete: Clear all session history through Settings > Danger Zone
  • Portability: Your data is stored in standard formats on your device
  • Revoke Permissions: Disable camera, notifications, or SMS access anytime through your device settings

11. Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:

  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines
  • Google Play Store Developer Policy
Summary: Lokd In respects your privacy. We built this app to keep your data entirely on your device. No servers, no tracking, no data collection. What happens on your phone, stays on your phone.
